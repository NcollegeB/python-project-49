Privacy

How BrainHacker uses data

BrainHacker keeps the information needed to run games, save account scores, remember interface preferences, and support advertising.

Accounts and game results

If you create an account, BrainHacker stores your username, a one-way password hash, and your saved game scores. Session cookies keep you signed in. Theme and sound preferences may be stored in your browser.

Advertising cookies

BrainHacker may use Google AdSense on game pages. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to BrainHacker and other websites. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to personalize ads when permitted. Ad services may also process IP addresses, browser or device identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Ads may involve Google-certified vendors and ad networks . You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings . You can also review industry opt-out choices at YourAdChoices .

Consent choices

Where configured and required, Google's advertising consent tools may present a message so visitors can accept, decline, or manage eligible data uses. Those choices may be stored so the message does not need to appear on every visit.
